Gorabels Privacy Policy

Effective 16 October 2026

Gorabels is a game by SumClarTech. This policy describes the current Android app, including its optional rewarded advertising.

Data saved on your device

The game saves scores, coins, unlocked routes, character and outfit choices, and settings in local app storage. It has no account or online leaderboard. You can reset local progress in Settings. Uninstalling the app can remove it.

Ads and third-party processing

On Android, Gorabels uses Google Mobile Ads to offer optional rewarded ads for a second chance or a one-run route preview. The Google Mobile Ads SDK and Google User Messaging Platform may process identifiers such as Advertising ID, device and app information, ad interactions, and consent choices to serve, measure, and protect ads. Availability and personalization depend on your device settings, location, and consent choices. The game requests consent information before requesting ads. Where required, Settings offers ad privacy choices. See Google's Privacy Policy for Google's handling of data.

Other network activity

The game bundles its art and fonts. During a run, an optional checkpoint trivia card can link to an external source; opening a link leaves the app and that site's policy applies. The app does not require camera or microphone access.

Children

Gorabels is intended for teens and adults, and is not directed to children under 13. If you are under the minimum age stated in its store listing, please do not use the app.

Changes

We may update this policy when the app or its services change. The effective date above will be updated.
